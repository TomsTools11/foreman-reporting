Notes

Data sources. GOAL inbound call report and call-level export, all campaigns, campaign launch through September 25, 2026. The first recorded call is June 8, 2026 and the most recent is September 25, 2026.

Call totals. Inbound calls are answered plus missed calls from the call report (46 + 83 = 129). The call-level export lists 139 records; 10 have a recorded duration of 0:00 and are not counted as calls.

Answered and missed. Splits classify each call in the call-level export as answered when it lasted 1 minute 30 seconds or longer. This reproduces the call report totals exactly: 129 calls, 46 answered, 30 quotes and 9 policies.

Scenarios. Section 03 applies the current answered-call rates (65.2% quoted, 19.6% bound) to 129 calls at 60% and 90% answered. Section 02 applies the same rates to the 83 missed calls.

Talk time. Sum of recorded durations on answered calls (16.3 hours).
