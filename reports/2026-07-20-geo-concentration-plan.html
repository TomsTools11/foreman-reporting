05

Recommendations

Proposed geographic targeting changes — to be confirmed in the July 20 session

1
Concentrate spend in seven counties
Bibb, DeKalb, Henry, Richmond, Whitfield, Cobb, Newton: combined 99 leads, 21 contacts (21.2% contact rate — 1.8× the blended average), 13 inbound calls, 4 quotes, 1 bound policy on $1,324.34 of spend (24.1%).
2
Hold Gwinnett, Clayton, Chatham and the four thin-sample counties
Gwinnett, Clayton, Chatham: 44 leads, 4 contacts, 5 inbound calls, 3 quotes. Rockdale, Columbia, Lowndes, Houston: 2+ contacts each on 5–6 leads — samples too thin to promote; held for more data.
3
Reduce where volume has not produced contact
Fulton county (46 leads, 1 contacted, $575.63) and Atlanta city (38 leads, 1 contacted, $449.08); Walton, Cherokee, Carroll (31 leads combined, 0 contacted). Secondary candidates: Coweta, Ware, Walker, Muscogee (27 leads combined, 0 contacted, $371.34).
4
Keep-set guardrails
The proposed keep-set retains 47 of 48 contacts (97.9%) and 41 of 47 inbound calls (87.2%) while removing the 8 reduce counties — 104 leads (24.9% of volume) and $1,311.98 of historical spend share (23.9%). Lead volume decreases by design: recent flow is 13–19 leads per day, and staffing capacity is the working constraint until new staff start.
5
Day-14 checkpoint
Review during the first week of August. Two metrics judge the test inside the keep-set: blended contact rate and inbound calls per week.
Once the set is confirmed, we apply the targeting changes in the platform. No geographic changes have been made as of this report; the July 16 delivery-start change (8:00 AM) is the only change currently live.

Notes

Exports pulled July 16 & July 20, 2026

Data sources: GOAL analytics all-time exports pulled July 16, 2026 — campaign summary, by county, and by city — plus the all-time inbound-calls export re-pulled July 20, 2026. Every figure in this report is recomputed from those exports; county rows sum exactly to the campaign totals (418 leads, 47 calls, 48 contacted, 17 quotes, 2 bound, $5,493.52).

Contact rate = leads contacted ÷ leads delivered. Inbound calls are counted separately from the lead funnel; the calls export contains 44 records against 47 calls in analytics (“Multiple Calls” records bundle repeat callers — detailed in the Inbound Call Timing report). City figures merge capitalization variants; 19 leads and 4 calls carry no city attribution. Contact rates on fewer than 10 leads are shown for reference only.

Tiers are a proposal for the July 20 working session; the confirmed set and budget level will be applied as geographic targeting changes in the platform.
