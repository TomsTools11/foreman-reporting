Updates and next steps

September 25, 2026

1
Outcomes since September 10
The September 25 outcome update covers 20 leads received September 7–23: 20 contacted, 15 quoted (including 8 in X-date follow-up) and 1 Auto policy bound. All-time totals now stand at 134 contacted, 57 quotes and 6 policies.
2
Home placements on the largest source
Over the last 30 days Tier 1 holds 17,904 of Home's 24,030 matched inventory (74.5%) and produced 510 ad placements; Tier 3 holds 3,153 (13.1%) and produced 325. We are reviewing Tier 1 competitiveness to convert more of that inventory into placements.
3
Weighting delivery toward the 11 targeted counties
All-time contact rate is 18.4% inside the targeted counties against 9.4% outside, and 4 of 5 policies came from inside. In the last 30 days of Home delivery 34 of 180 leads (18.9%) came from targeted counties. We are reviewing county-level delivery with the platform team.
4
Inbound call handling
129 inbound calls since launch, 46 answered (35.7%). Answered calls produced 30 quotes and 9 policies. At a 60% answer rate the same rates produce about 50 quotes and 15 policies; at 90%, about 76 quotes and 23 policies. Detail is in the Inbound Call Performance report.

Notes

Period. Campaign launch through September 25, 2026, both campaigns blended.

Outcome data. Contact, quote and policy figures in sections 01–04 reflect outcomes recorded in GOAL as of September 25, 2026. Sections 05, 06 and 08 use county and source exports pulled before the September 25 outcome update registered, so they reflect outcomes through September 10. Leads, inbound calls, clicks, searches and spend are current through September 25, 2026.

Rates. Contact rate is contacted leads divided by leads. Quote rate in the funnel is quotes divided by contacted leads; where quote rate is shown against leads it is labelled as such. Inbound calls are counted separately from the lead funnel and are not included in contact or quote rates.

Reconciliation. Totals are taken from the by-source export; the by-county export reconciles to the same leads, outcomes and spend. Home and Auto figures in section 04 come from each campaign's all-time source export. Section 07 has not been refreshed and shows the September 10 property-type export. September 10 values in section 02 use the same by-source basis.

Call counts. Section 09 uses the call-tracking export, which records 129 calls since launch. The campaign exports record 108 calls on a different attribution basis; that figure is used everywhere else in this report.

Geography. County targeting has been live since July 20, 2026. All-time county figures include delivery from before that date. 159 Georgia counties recorded activity.

Matched inventory. Matched inventory (section 09) is the count of shopper searches on each source that matched the campaign's targeting. The campaign enters an auction on a subset of that inventory, and ad placements are the auctions won. Shopper searches in sections 01 through 04 count auctions entered.
