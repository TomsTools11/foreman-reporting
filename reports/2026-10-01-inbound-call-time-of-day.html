Notes

Two call sources. Section 01 all-time figures come from call tracking: 132 inbound calls, 47 answered, 85 missed, 31 quotes and 9 policies, with answered calls as recorded by call tracking. Call tracking has no time stamps, so hour and weekday views use the caller list.

Caller list. 99 callers, one row per caller at the first call (82 completed, 14 with multiple calls, 3 no-answer). 68 came in on the Home campaign and 31 on Auto. The caller list has no answered flag, so a call of 60 seconds or more counts as answered: 42 of 99 callers (42.4%). Because the caller list counts callers and call tracking counts calls, the two answer rates differ.

Time zone. GOAL records times in Pacific time. All times here are converted to Eastern (Pacific plus 3 hours).

Quotes and policies. The last-30-day quote and policy counts are those recorded on the caller list. The caller list records 19 quotes and 1 policy all-time against 31 and 9 in call tracking, so the 30-day counts may be incomplete.

Campaign exports. The campaign exports count 110 inbound calls on a different attribution basis. Those figures are not used in this report.
