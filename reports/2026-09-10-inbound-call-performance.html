Notes

Data sources. GOAL inbound call report and call-level export, all campaigns, campaign launch through September 10, 2026. The first recorded call is June 8, 2026 and the most recent is September 9, 2026. Every figure on this page is recomputed from those two files.

Call totals. Inbound calls are answered plus missed calls from the call report (42 + 74 = 116). The call-level export lists 125 records; 9 have a recorded duration of 0:00 and are not counted as calls.

Answered and missed. Weekly, monthly, weekday and call-length splits classify each call in the call-level export as answered when it lasted 1 minute 30 seconds or longer. This reproduces the call report totals exactly: 116 calls, 42 answered, 26 quotes and 8 policies.

Quotes and policies. Quotes and policies on this page are those recorded on inbound calls. They are counted separately from the lead funnel in the campaign reports.

Call counts. The campaign reports record 96 inbound calls since launch on a different attribution basis. 11 callers placed more than one call (26 of the 116 calls).

Talk time. Talk time is the sum of recorded durations on answered calls (15.0 hours). The call report rounds its talk-time field to whole hours per row.
