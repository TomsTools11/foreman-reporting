Notes

Exports pulled July 30, 2026

Data sources: GOAL analytics all-time and trailing-30-day campaign exports pulled July 30, 2026 for the call counts (64 all-time, 29 in the last 30 days), and the inbound call disposition export returned July 30, 2026 for call-level outcomes (42 records, June 8 – July 30, 2026). Every figure on this page is recomputed from those files.

42 of the 64 calls carry a returned disposition. Reached, quoted, and bound rates on this page are calculated against those 42 records, not against all 64 calls; the 22 calls without a returned disposition have no recorded outcome either way. Reached means the disposition indicates a conversation took place: Contacted, Quoted with Contact, or Sold. Attempted Contact is not counted as reached.

The export records call duration and a call status of Completed, Multiple Calls, or blank, but no answered-or-missed field. Median call length is 2 minutes 41 seconds across the 42 records; one record has no duration and one records zero seconds. Inbound calls are counted separately from the lead funnel and are not included in lead, contact, or quote counts elsewhere in this report set.
