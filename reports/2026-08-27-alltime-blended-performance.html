09

Updates and next steps

August 27, 2026

1
Auto paused, budget consolidated into Home
Effective August 11, 2026. Over the full account history Auto matched 33,871 shopper searches and was placed on 2.8% of them, against 12.7% for Home on 23,350 searches. Consolidating the budget puts it behind the campaign with the higher placement rate.
2
Raising Home competitiveness on the largest source
Over the last 30 days Tier 1 holds 14,415 of Home's 20,512 matched inventory (70%) and produced 486 ad placements; Tier 3 holds 3,834 and produced 657. We are increasing Home's competitiveness on Tier 1 to convert more of that inventory into placements.
3
Weighting delivery toward the 11 targeted counties
Contact rate inside the targeted counties is 20.4% against 10.2% outside, cost per contacted lead is $62.14 against $125.65, and three of the four policies bound came from inside. We are reviewing county-level delivery with the platform team.
4
Bringing outcome data current
Contact, quote and policy counts depend on CRM outcome data being imported into the platform. We are loading outcomes for leads delivered since July 31 and will restate the conversion figures in this report once that import completes.
5
Inbound call handling
42 inbound calls in the last 30 days, 15 answered (35.7%). All-time: 109 calls, 40 answered (36.7%), 24 quotes and 7 policies from answered calls. Timing detail is in the Inbound Calls by Time of Day report.

Notes

Period. Campaign launch through August 27, 2026, both campaigns blended. Home ran throughout; Auto was paused August 11, 2026.

Outcome data. Contacted, quote and policy counts come from CRM outcome data imported into the platform and are updated each time an import runs. Outcomes for the most recently delivered leads may not yet be loaded, so these three counts should be read as a floor rather than a final figure. Leads, inbound calls, clicks, searches, ad placements and spend are current through August 27, 2026.

Rates. Contact rate is contacted leads divided by leads. Quote rate in the funnel is quotes divided by contacted leads; where quote rate is shown against leads it is labelled as such. Inbound calls are counted separately from the lead funnel and are not included in contact or quote rates.

Reconciliation. Totals are taken from the campaign performance export. The by-source and by-county exports reconcile to the same totals as of August 27, 2026. On the July 16, 2026 export the by-source total ran 9 leads and $159.14 above the campaign total, from leads with no source attribution; section 02 uses the by-source basis on both dates so the two are directly comparable.

Call counts. Section 09 uses the call-tracking export, which records 109 calls all-time and 42 in the last 30 days. The campaign export records 91 calls all-time on a different attribution basis; that figure is used everywhere else in this report.

Geography. County targeting has been live since July 20, 2026. All-time county figures include delivery from before that date. 159 Georgia counties recorded activity.

Matched inventory. Matched inventory is the count of shopper searches on each source that matched the campaign's targeting. The campaign enters an auction on a subset of that inventory, and ad placements are the auctions won. The shopper-search figure in section 01 counts auctions entered, which is why it is smaller than total matched inventory.

Source exports. The underlying CSV exports accompany this report.
