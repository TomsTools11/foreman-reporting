Updates and next steps

September 10, 2026

1
Home is the active campaign
Auto has been paused since August 11, 2026, with its budget moved into Home. Over the account history Auto was placed on 2.8% of 33,871 shopper searches and Home on 12.3% of 27,605.
2
Raising Home placements on the largest source
Over the last 30 days Tier 1 holds 15,539 of Home's 21,783 matched inventory (71%) and produced 481 ad placements; Tier 3 holds 3,703 (17%) and produced 513. We are reviewing Tier 1 competitiveness to convert more of that inventory into placements.
3
Weighting delivery toward the 11 targeted counties
All-time contact rate is 19.3% inside the targeted counties against 10.5% outside, and 4 of 5 policies came from inside. In the last 30 days of Home delivery the split is 21.4% against 14.3%, with both policies inside. We are reviewing county-level delivery with the platform team.
4
Inbound call handling
116 inbound calls since launch, 42 answered (36.2%). Answered calls produced 26 quotes and 8 policies. Weekly, weekday and talk-time detail is in the Inbound Call Performance report.

Notes

Period. Campaign launch through September 10, 2026, both campaigns blended. Home ran throughout; Auto was paused August 11, 2026.

Outcome data. Contact, quote and policy figures reflect outcomes recorded in GOAL as of September 10, 2026. Leads, inbound calls, clicks, searches and spend are current through September 10, 2026.

Rates. Contact rate is contacted leads divided by leads. Quote rate in the funnel is quotes divided by contacted leads; where quote rate is shown against leads it is labelled as such. Inbound calls are counted separately from the lead funnel and are not included in contact or quote rates.

Reconciliation. Totals are taken from the by-source export; the by-county export reconciles to the same leads, outcomes and spend. Home and Auto figures in section 04 come from the property-type export, where Auto appears as the unassigned row. August 27 values in section 02 use the same by-source basis.

Call counts. Section 09 uses the call-tracking export, which records 116 calls since launch. The campaign exports record 96 calls on a different attribution basis; that figure is used everywhere else in this report.

Geography. County targeting has been live since July 20, 2026. All-time county figures include delivery from before that date. 159 Georgia counties recorded activity.

Matched inventory. Matched inventory (section 09) is the count of shopper searches on each source that matched the campaign's targeting. The campaign enters an auction on a subset of that inventory, and ad placements are the auctions won. Shopper searches in sections 01 through 04 count auctions entered.
