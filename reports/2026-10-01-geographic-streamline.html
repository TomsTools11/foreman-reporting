Notes

Period. Campaign launch through October 2, 2026, Home and Auto blended. The Auto campaign was paused on August 11, 2026 and is paused today; the lead list shows 9 Auto leads on September 24 and 25, 2026.

Outcome data. Contact, quote and policy figures reflect outcomes recorded in GOAL as of October 2, 2026, after the latest outcome import.

Reconciliation. The county export totals 1,000 leads, 149 contacted, 57 quotes, 6 policies and $12,918.41 of spend, matching the campaign total exactly.

Rates. Contact rate is contacted leads divided by leads. Quote rate is quotes divided by leads.

Rules. Rule A keeps counties with 10+ leads and a contact rate of 18% or higher. Rule B keeps counties with 10+ leads and a contact rate of 16% or higher, plus any county with a bound policy.

ZIP codes. ZIP figures come from the lead list (1,002 records against 1,000 leads; every record carries a ZIP code; 375 ZIP codes). The lead list carries no spend. ZIP codes are matched to counties with the GeoNames postal database and checked against a USPS ZIP table; where the two disagree (20 ZIP codes), the county that matches GOAL's county records is used. After matching, 125 of 131 counties match the county export exactly; the remaining 14 records sit in ZIP codes that cross county lines, mostly 30052 (Loganville, Gwinnett and Walton). Some ZIP codes cross county lines, so an inclusion list built from ZIP codes can reach a small number of shoppers in a neighboring county.

Outcomes by ZIP. The lead list records 55 of 149 contacted leads, 20 of 57 quotes and 1 of 6 policies, because outcomes imported in bulk do not write back to it. ZIP rows therefore show leads only; contacted, quoted and bound figures are shown at county level from the county export.
