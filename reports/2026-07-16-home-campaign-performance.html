Inbound calls route to a dedicated line and ring round-robin until answered. Campaign-to-date through July 2, 14 of 43 inbound calls were answered (32.6%); the target is 80%+.

Base bid right-priced to ~$7.00
The base bid moved from $6.51 to ~$7.00 to hold the anchor tier above its 2nd-position market rate. Tier 1 and Tier 2 modifiers adjusted (150% → 140%, 115% → 110%) so their effective bids stay at current placement levels; Home Search B holds at 125%.
4
Budget concentrates on Home
Home carries both of the account's bound policies and holds its daily budget as Auto steps down toward ~$65/day.
