Notes

Period. Trailing 30 days ending August 27, 2026, Auto campaign, Georgia. The campaign was paused on August 11, 2026, so the window contains 14 delivering days. The comparison window is the trailing 30 days ending July 16, 2026, which contains 30 delivering days. Per-day figures are calculated on delivering days only.

All-time. Section 04 covers campaign launch through August 11, 2026, the last day of Auto delivery.

Outcome data. Contacted, quote and policy counts come from CRM outcome data imported into the platform and are updated each time an import runs. Outcomes for leads delivered in the most recent weeks have not yet been imported, so those counts should be read as a floor rather than a final figure.

Matched inventory. Matched inventory is the count of shopper searches on each source that matched the campaign's targeting. The campaign enters an auction on a subset of that inventory, and ad placements are the auctions won. The shopper-search figure in section 01 counts auctions entered, which is why it is smaller than total matched inventory.

Rates. Contact rate is contacted leads divided by leads. Funnel percentages are relative to the stage above. Inbound calls are counted separately from the lead funnel.

Source exports. The underlying CSV exports accompany this report.
