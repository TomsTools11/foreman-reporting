Updates & recommendations

July 16, 2026

1
Lead delivery start time moved to 8:00 a.m.
Applied to every weekday on July 16. Stop times unchanged. Early-morning inbound call volume will be reviewed at the next session.
2
Concentrate spend on the highest contact-rate counties
Bibb, DeKalb, Henry, Richmond, and Whitfield combine for 64 leads at a 23.4% blended contact rate with 9 inbound calls on $903.73 of spend. Cobb and Newton follow at 16.7–17.6%. Geography set and budget level to be confirmed in the July 20 session.
3
Reduce exposure where volume has not produced contact
Fulton: 46 leads, 1 contacted (2.2%), $575.63 of spend (10.5% of total). Atlanta city: 38 leads, 1 contacted, $449.08. Walton, Cherokee, and Carroll: 31 leads combined, 0 contacted.
4
Working session scheduled
Monday, July 20, 1:30–2:30 p.m. CT: confirm the narrowed geography set, set the budget for the concentration test, and review inbound-call timing since the 8:00 a.m. change.

Data source: GOAL analytics all-time exports, blended across all campaigns, pulled July 16, 2026 — campaign summary, by county, by city, by consumer age, by number of drivers, by number of vehicles, and by property age.

City figures merge capitalization variants of the same city name. Property age is captured on Home leads; driver and vehicle counts are captured on Auto leads. Counties and cities below the stated lead minimums are aggregated in the table footnotes rather than ranked, and rates on fewer than 10 leads are shown for reference only.
