Notes

Period. Trailing 30 days ending September 10, 2026, Home campaign, Georgia. The comparison window is the trailing 30 days ending August 27, 2026, as published in the August 27 report. Both windows are 30 days and they overlap.

Outcome data. Contact, quote and policy figures reflect outcomes recorded in GOAL as of September 10, 2026. Leads, inbound calls, clicks, searches and spend are current through September 10, 2026.

Matched inventory and placement rate. Matched inventory is the count of shopper searches on each source that matched the campaign's targeting. The campaign enters an auction on a subset of that inventory, and ad placements are the auctions won. The shopper-search figure in section 01 counts auctions entered, which is why it is smaller than total matched inventory. Placement rate is ad placements divided by matched inventory on that source.

Rates. Contact rate is contacted leads divided by leads. Quote percentages in the funnel are relative to the stage above. Inbound calls are counted separately from the lead funnel.

Reconciliation. Totals are taken from the by-source export; the by-county, consumer-age and property-type exports reconcile to the same leads, outcomes and spend. Source-level inventory and placements in section 04 come from the source settings export, which can differ from the by-source export by a few placements.

Geography. County targeting has been live since July 20, 2026, so this window falls entirely after that change. 154 Georgia counties recorded activity in the window.
