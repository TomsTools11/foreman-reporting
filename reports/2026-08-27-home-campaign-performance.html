Updates and next steps

August 27, 2026

1
Auto budget consolidated into Home
Effective August 11, 2026. Home spend rose from $83.91 to $89.49 per day across the two windows and lead volume rose from 6.7 to 7.4 per day.
2
Raising competitiveness on Tier 1
Tier 1 holds 14,415 of the campaign's 20,512 matched inventory (70%) but converts only 3.4% of it into ad placements, against 17.1% on Tier 3. Once placed, Tier 1 is clicked 29.8% of the time against 17.5% for Tier 3. Raising Tier 1's placement rate is the largest available source of additional volume in the campaign.
3
Weighting delivery toward the 11 targeted counties
Contact rate is 19.1% inside the targeted counties against 9.2% outside, and cost per contacted lead is $66.74 against $130.26. Those counties currently take 22.4% of spend. We are reviewing county-level delivery with the platform team.
4
Bringing outcome data current
Quote and policy counts for this window are not final. We are importing the outstanding CRM outcomes and will restate the affected rows once that import completes.
5
Inbound calls
29 inbound calls in this window against 18 in the prior window. Call-tracking records 42 calls and 15 answered (35.7%) over the same period across both campaigns. Timing detail is in the Inbound Calls by Time of Day report.

Notes

Period. Trailing 30 days ending August 27, 2026, Home campaign, Georgia. The comparison window is the trailing 30 days ending July 16, 2026. Both windows are 30 days, so daily averages are directly comparable.

Outcome data. Contacted, quote and policy counts come from CRM outcome data imported into the platform and are updated each time an import runs. Outcomes for leads delivered in the most recent weeks have not yet been imported, so those three counts for the latest window should be read as a floor rather than a final figure. Leads, inbound calls, clicks, searches, ad placements and spend are current through August 27, 2026.

Matched inventory and placement rate. Matched inventory is the count of shopper searches on each source that matched the campaign's targeting. The campaign enters an auction on a subset of that inventory, and ad placements are the auctions won. The shopper-search figure in section 01 counts auctions entered, which is why it is smaller than total matched inventory. Placement rate is ad placements divided by matched inventory on that source.

Rates. Contact rate is contacted leads divided by leads. Quote percentages in the funnel are relative to the stage above. Inbound calls are counted separately from the lead funnel.

Geography. County targeting has been live since July 20, 2026, so this window falls entirely after that change. 155 Georgia counties recorded activity in the window.

Source exports. The underlying CSV exports accompany this report.
