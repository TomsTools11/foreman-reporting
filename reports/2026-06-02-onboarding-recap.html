Why your GOAL leads are different

Your GOAL leads are exclusive — each ad runs specifically for Foreman Agency, and the leads it generates are never resold to another agency, unlike aggregator leads sold to four or five agents at once. Even better, you're competing for that shopper at the very top of the funnel — in the same auction the MediaAlphas and SmartFinancials are bidding in. We also know each prospect clicked your ad before submitting. Shoppers often request quotes on more than one site, which is exactly why reaching out fast — especially to inbound calls — is the single biggest lever on whether they answer and close.

Your follow-up playbook

The setup is on us. Conversion is about how the leads get worked — this is the routine that turns exclusive leads into policies.

1
Answer inbound calls first — every time
When someone finishes your form and taps Call Now, that's the highest-intent lead there is. With three producers, the rule is simple: whoever's free grabs it immediately. A GOAL inbound call is the top priority in the day.
2
Work every lead across 30 days, not two calls
Most agents stop after two or three attempts — often right before the lead would have answered. A structured cadence over the first 30 days is what separates leads that fizzle from leads that close.

Phone, text, and email together. Plenty of prospects who never pick up will reply to a text — and that reply is the opening to the conversation.

Before every review, export your quote/bind and disposition data from Lead Manager and send it over. That's how we calculate your true cost per policy and steer budget toward what actually closes. Without it, we're optimizing blind.
